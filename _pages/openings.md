## Openings and Recruitment

Our group primarily recruits undergraduate students from Northeastern University at Qinhuangdao (NEUQ) for research internships. We also welcome research visits and collaborations with students and researchers from other universities and institutions.

Outside the annual recruitment period, exceptional candidates and potential collaborators are also welcome to contact us regarding suitable research opportunities.

## What We Look For

We are looking for students who are genuinely interested in scientific research and demonstrate:

* Strong self-motivation and intellectual curiosity;
* The ability to learn independently and solve unfamiliar problems;
* A responsible and collaborative attitude;
* A willingness to devote consistent time and effort to research over an extended period.

Research requires patience, persistence, and careful experimentation. Applicants should be prepared to read academic literature, reproduce existing methods, conduct rigorous experiments, analyze unexpected results, and communicate their progress regularly.

## Technical Background

Applicants are expected to have a solid foundation in mathematics, programming, or machine learning. We particularly welcome students who fit one or both of the following profiles:

### Theory- and Research-Oriented Candidates

You should have a strong mathematical foundation and a sound understanding of deep learning. You are expected to have independently implemented and trained commonly used deep-learning models rather than only calling existing APIs. During the internship, you should be willing to investigate research questions through both theoretical analysis and carefully designed experiments.

### Engineering- and Experiment-Oriented Candidates

You should possess strong programming and engineering skills, preferably with previous project or research experience related to deep learning. You should be comfortable implementing models, managing experiments, debugging code, and analyzing results. During the internship, you will also be expected to look beyond empirical performance and develop a deeper understanding of why a method works—or fails.

Prior publications are not required. However, evidence of independent learning, hands-on implementation, research potential, and sustained commitment will be highly valued.
